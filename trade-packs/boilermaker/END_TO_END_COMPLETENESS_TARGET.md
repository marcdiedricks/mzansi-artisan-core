# Boilermaker End-to-End Completeness Target

**MAF stage:** MAF-02-09  
**Trade Pack:** ZA-BOILERMAKER-001  
**Status:** CONTROLLED TARGET — build gaps remain

## Purpose

Define what the Boilermaker Artisan Journey Companion must cover before MAF may describe the product scope as complete.

This target is based on the current registered South African qualification baseline and must remain subject to source provenance and change control.

## Current qualification baseline

- National Occupational Certificate: Boilermaker
- SAQA ID: 123381
- Curriculum: 651302-000-01-00
- NQF Level: 4
- Minimum credits: 540
- Components: Knowledge + Practical Skill + Work Experience
- External assessment: required through the relevant QCTO Assessment Quality Partner

The legacy SAQA 93626 qualification remains separate and must never be silently mixed into current content.

**14 Knowledge Modules / 162 credits**

Current MAF position:

MAF product rule: the Companion may prepare, explain, organise evidence and support supervised learning. It does not authorise hazardous practical work or certify practical competence.

### C. Work Experience component

**5 Work Experience Modules / 195 credits**

1. WM-01 — Pattern and template development processes — 35 credits
2. WM-02 — Tools, equipment and machinery maintenance and operation processes — 30 credits
3. WM-03 — Workshop/on-site cutting and welding processes — 45 credits
4. WM-04 — Fabrication, assembling and repairing of metal components and structures — 45 credits
5. WM-05 — Assembling and erecting processes for structures and components — 40 credits

MAF product rule: workplace experience must be recorded/prepared for human/institutional verification. Local app records are not themselves formal workplace competence decisions.

### D. RPL pathway

The Companion must support the learner's understanding and preparation for applicable RPL routes, including:

- RPL for access to training
- RPL for exemption of modules where formally applicable
- RPL preparation for access to external assessment where applicable
- identification and organisation of evidence
- identification of learning/training gaps

All formal RPL decisions remain with authorised providers/quality partners.

### E. Assessment / qualification pathway

The Companion must clearly distinguish:

1. in-app learning checks and revision;
2. formal internal assessment and Statements of Results;
3. completion of required practical modules;
4. record of completed work experience;
5. Portfolio of Evidence where required;
6. eligibility/admission to the External Integrated Summative Assessment (EISA);
7. formal external assessment through the relevant authorised QCTO/AQP system;
8. formal qualification/certification outcome outside the app.

In-app quiz results must never be represented as EISA, trade-test, competence or certification results.

## Learner journey target

The completed Companion must support this journey without claiming institutional authority:

**ENTER TRADE → UNDERSTAND QUALIFICATION → LEARN KNOWLEDGE → PREPARE FOR SUPERVISED PRACTICAL SKILLS → RECORD/ORGANISE WORKPLACE EXPERIENCE → BUILD/ORGANISE EVIDENCE → RPL SUPPORT WHERE APPLICABLE → INTERNAL/FORMAL REQUIREMENTS AWARENESS → EXTERNAL ASSESSMENT PREPARATION → QUALIFICATION/CERTIFICATION PATHWAY → CONTINUING ARTISAN SUPPORT**

## Product completeness gates

MAF may only mark the Boilermaker Companion COMPLETE when all mandatory gates pass:

### Gate 1 — Authority
Current qualification, curriculum, assessment and governance sources verified and provenance recorded.

### Gate 2 — Knowledge
All required Knowledge Modules are covered by verified learner content and learning checks.

### Gate 3 — Practical preparation
All Practical Skill Modules have appropriate safe learner-support coverage, without replacing supervised practical training or assessment.

### Gate 4 — Workplace journey
All Work Experience Modules have appropriate learner guidance/evidence organisation support without false competence claims.

### Gate 5 — RPL / assessment journey
RPL and formal assessment pathways are correctly represented and clearly separated from in-app learning.

### Gate 6 — Safety
Every learner-facing component passes the MAF safety tier and boundary audit.

### Gate 7 — Provenance/media/localisation
Sources, media rights/status, terminology and approved language/localisation scope pass their audits.

### Gate 8 — Technical
Offline, local state, progress, evidence, backup/recovery, navigation, ordinary-Android usability and PWA acceptance tests pass.

### Gate 9 — Completeness audit
The Trade Completeness Matrix contains no unresolved mandatory GAP, REJECT-without-replacement, failed verification or failed technical acceptance item.

### Gate 10 — Pilot freeze
Real-device/pilot evidence reviewed; only then may the approved product scope be frozen.
