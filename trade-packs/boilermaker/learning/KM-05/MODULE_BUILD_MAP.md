## Source control

Primary source: QCTO occupational curriculum hosted by CHIETA, Curriculum Document Template OQD-DT-02, Version 1.2, Review Date Sept 2024, KM-05 section.

MAF rule: this build map records the curriculum requirements. It does not claim accreditation and does not authorise practical joining, welding, lifting, hoisting or slinging work.

## Module purpose

KM-05 develops knowledge needed to understand assembling and joining of components and structures, including use of drawings/specifications, joining methods, layout/fit concepts and lifting/hoisting/slinging knowledge.

## Five-topic learner architecture

### Lesson 1 — Joining Simple Components
**Curriculum:** KM-05-KT01 — 15%

Required topic elements:
- terminology, concepts and principles of joining
- drawings, codes and specifications used in joining
- hazards, risks and safety pertaining to joining
- assembling and fastening for simple component assembly
- layout and fit practices for simple components

Learner boundary: knowledge, recognition and interpretation only. No unsupervised hazardous joining procedure.

### Lesson 2 — Joining Steel Structures
**Curriculum:** KM-05-KT02 — 25%

The learner lesson must cover the curriculum's steel-structure joining knowledge and be traceable to the official topic elements and IACs before freeze.

Assessment focus includes identifying types/characteristics/applications of steel structures and describing appropriate joining methods, advantages and limitations.

### Lesson 3 — Joining Complex Assemblies
**Curriculum:** KM-05-KT03 — 30%

Required topic elements:
- concepts and principles of joining complex assemblies
- assembling and fastening methods for complex assemblies
- layout and fit of complex assemblies

Assessment remains knowledge/formative only and must not claim practical competence.

### Lesson 4 — Lifting Loads
**Curriculum:** KM-05-KT04 — 15%

Required assessment knowledge includes:
- recognition/description of overhead-crane hand signals
- safety precautions, processes and equipment
- interpretation of load-chart data
- working-load calculations

Safety boundary: theoretical preparation only. The PWA does not authorise lifting operations or replace competent supervision/training.

### Lesson 5 — Hoisting, Lifting and Slinging
**Curriculum:** KM-05-KT05 — 15%

Required topic element:
- hoisting, lifting and slinging techniques and safety

Required assessment knowledge includes:
- identifying/describing/selecting lifting equipment for a task at knowledge level
- working-load calculations
- safety precautions, processes and equipment

Safety boundary: recognition, concepts and calculations only. No unsupervised operational instructions.
